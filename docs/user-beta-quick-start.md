# Mercy Legal AI Beta Attorney Quick Start

This guide is for invited D.C. attorneys testing Mercy in beta.

## Before You Start

Mercy is an AI assistant for attorney-supervised legal work. Do not treat Mercy output as final legal advice, verified authority, or verified record support. You must review all drafts, citations, quotations, procedural facts, and legal conclusions before use.

Do not upload real client-confidential documents unless the beta invitation explicitly confirms that production data handling, retention, deletion, and support terms are active for your tenant.

## What Mercy Can Help With

- Open a D.C. matter.
- Capture structured intake facts.
- Identify missing context.
- Research D.C.-focused questions with source-grounding metadata.
- Draft attorney-review-required legal text.
- Review documents or selected Word text.
- Generate D.C. template-based drafts.
- Surface citation, record, confidentiality, and ethics warnings.

## Basic Workflow

1. Sign in to the Mercy web app.
2. Create or select a matter.
3. Complete intake with parties, posture, forum, deadlines, facts, requested relief, and sensitivity flags.
4. Upload or attach only approved test documents.
5. Ask a D.C. research question or drafting request.
6. Review the reliability panel.
7. Verify all citations, source text, facts, and record references.
8. Copy, export, or use the draft only after attorney review.

## Word Add-in Workflow

1. Open Word.
2. Open the Mercy taskpane.
3. Select text or use the active document.
4. Choose analysis, explanation, drafting, citation check, or template generation.
5. Review route, source, guardrail, and attorney-review metadata.
6. Insert or copy output only after review.

## What the Reliability Signals Mean

- **Route**: The legal workflow Mercy selected.
- **Confidence**: Router certainty, not legal correctness.
- **Citations**: Candidate source support or placeholders requiring verification.
- **Guardrails**: Ethics, confidentiality, citation, record, and attorney-review checks.
- **Fallback**: The core, model, source, or Word API was unavailable or limited.
- **Trace ID**: Internal troubleshooting reference.

## Beta Feedback

Use the feedback control after each workflow. Helpful feedback includes:

- What you were trying to do.
- Whether the output was useful.
- Whether source/citation status was clear.
- Whether the workflow felt confusing.
- Whether any output looked overconfident or unsafe.
